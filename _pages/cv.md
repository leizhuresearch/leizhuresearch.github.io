## Research interests

- **Mechanics of hybrid materials:** nonlinear mechanical response, fracture and lattice anharmonicity in inorganic–organic hybrid materials.
- **Machine learning for materials:** accurate, transferable machine-learned interatomic potentials and active-learning strategies for materials simulation.
- **Multiscale modelling:** combining quantum-mechanical, QM/MM and machine-learning methods to predict materials properties across length and time scales.
- **Defects, interfaces and degradation:** atomic-scale mechanisms that govern performance and long-term stability in semiconductors and energy materials, in close collaboration with experiment.

## Research experience

**Postdoctoral Research Associate**, Department of Materials, University of Oxford (2023 – present)<br>
Atomistic and machine-learning modelling of the mechanical, interfacial and defect properties of halide perovskites for solar cells.

- Lead computational contributor in collaborations with experimental groups at Imperial College London and the University of Manchester, using DFT and ab initio molecular dynamics to explain how molecular additives work at perovskite interfaces.
- Development of machine-learned interatomic potentials (MACE) with committee-based active learning for the mechanical properties of halide perovskites.

**PhD Researcher**, Department of Chemistry, University College London (2017 – 2022)<br>
Multiscale modelling of intrinsic point defects in the wide-band-gap nitride AlN.

- Derived a classical interatomic potential for wurtzite AlN and used it to identify interstitialcy migration mechanisms and revise vacancy migration barriers.
- Applied hybrid QM/MM embedded-cluster methods (ChemShell) to establish the donor and acceptor character of intrinsic defects in AlN.
- Calculated optical absorption, emission and carrier-capture behaviour of intrinsic defects, assigning specific defects to measured absorption and luminescence bands.
